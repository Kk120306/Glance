Design System

Glance

A communication companion for motor-impaired patients — spoken through the eyes. Warm, calm, and built so a glance is enough.

👁
Eyes first

Every action is reachable by gaze, blink, or a single vocalization. No hands required, ever.

🫧
A companion, not a console

The Blob Agent carries emotion and guidance so the interface never feels clinical.

◐
High contrast, low load

Large targets, generous space, one decision at a time. Calm by default.

♥
Dignity & speed

Help and SOS are always one dwell away — fast when it matters, gentle the rest of the time.

Color

A warm cream canvas keeps the screen calm and glare-free; violet leads, with teal and pink for affirmation and warmth. Semantic colors stay reserved for moments that matter.

Motion

Motion is slow and organic — breathing, floating, filling. Nothing flashes. Urgency is the only fast motion, reserved entirely for SOS.

Breathe · 4.5s

Idle scale 1 → 1.045. Signals "alive and waiting" without demanding attention.

Dwell fill · 1.8s

Linear ring fill maps look-time to commitment, with a soft confirm at completion.

Urgent pulse · 1.1s

The only quick rhythm. Red halo expands and fades — used exclusively for SOS.
